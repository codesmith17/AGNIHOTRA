Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026 · Applies to the EternalAgni Android app (com.eternalagni.app) and the EternalAgni website.

The short version

EternalAgni has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics. We do not run a server that stores anything about you. Your saved places, reminder settings and preferences stay on your device.

What stays on your device

Agnihotra sunrise and sunset timings are calculated entirely on your device using the NOAA solar position algorithm. The following are stored locally in the app and are never transmitted to us:

  • Your chosen location and any saved places (name, latitude, longitude)
  • Reminder, notification, sound and language preferences
  • Diagnostic logs the app keeps for troubleshooting (pruned after 30 days)

Uninstalling the app removes all of this. You can also clear individual saved places and reset preferences from within the app at any time.

Location

Accurate Agnihotra timings depend on your geographic coordinates. Granting location access is optional — you can instead search for a place by name or pick a point on the map, and the app works fully without the permission.

If you do use location, or search for a place, coordinates or your search text are sent to:

  • Nominatim (OpenStreetMap Foundation) and Photon (Komoot) — to turn coordinates into a place name, and place names into coordinates.
  • OpenStreetMap tile servers — to draw the map when you pick a place visually.

These requests are made over HTTPS, are used only to answer that one request, and carry no account or advertising identifier. We do not receive a copy of them and we do not store your location on any server. Those services handle the request under their own privacy policies (OpenStreetMap Foundation, Komoot).

Other network connections

  • Update checks. The app asks GitHub whether a newer release is available. This sends only a standard web request; no personal data is attached.
  • PDF schedule export. Generating a PDF fetches a QR code image for the EternalAgni website address. The QR code contains only that public website link — none of your data.

All network traffic from the app uses HTTPS.

Support reports

If you tap “Share Support Report”, the app builds a diagnostic file and hands it to the email or sharing app you choose. Nothing is sent automatically — you decide whether to send it, to whom, and you can review or delete the attachment first. If you do email us, we use the report and your email address only to answer your support request.

Permissions and why they are used

  • Location — optional; to compute timings for where you are.
  • Notifications — to deliver your sunrise and sunset reminders.
  • Alarms and reminders — Agnihotra must be performed at an exact moment, so reminders are scheduled as exact alarms.
  • Full-screen notifications — to show the countdown on your lock screen at ritual time, if you enable it.
  • Run at startup — to restore your scheduled reminders after the device reboots.
  • Ignore battery optimisations — optional; you are asked only if reminders are being delayed by power saving.

Children

EternalAgni is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact
