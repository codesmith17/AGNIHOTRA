        Sunrise and sunset mantras are included as a quick practice reference.

        Sunrise Mantra

        Sūrya Mantra
        0:00 / 0:00

        सूर्याय स्वाहा । सूर्याय इदं न मम ।

        प्रजापतये स्वाहा । प्रजापतये इदं न मम ॥

        Sūryāya Svāhā | Sūryāya Idaṃ Na Mama |

        Prajāpataye Svāhā | Prajāpataye Idaṃ Na Mama ||

        "To the Sun, salutations. This is not mine, but Yours.

        To Prajapati (Lord of Creation), salutations. This is not mine, but Yours."

        Sunset Mantra

        Agni Mantra
        0:00 / 0:00

        अग्नये स्वाहा । अग्नये इदं न मम ।

        प्रजापतये स्वाहा । प्रजापतये इदं न मम ॥

        Agnaye Svāhā | Agnaye Idaṃ Na Mama |

        Prajāpataye Svāhā | Prajāpataye Idaṃ Na Mama ||

        "To Fire (Agni), salutations. This is not mine, but Yours.

        To Prajapati (Lord of Creation), salutations. This is not mine, but Yours."
